What is writer's block? 

There are many times that I sat down to write and could not think of anything _worthy_ of writing. 

_Worthy_. Yes. 

Something _worthy_ of writing. 

Something _worthy_ that I could put my name on and publish.

I believe that **the need for your writing to be worthy** could be one of the reasons for the writer's block. 

This is my writing process.

1. **Writing for myself** - The act of translating my thoughts into words. Those could be random thoughts or something intentional. 
2. **Editing for others** - Editing what I wrote to form a coherent train of thought, natural flow of ideas or stories. 

A basic ingredient of writing is a thought. Agreed? 

Most of us have a continuous stream of thoughts, memories, internal monologues, imagery, etc in our minds most of the time. So, at any moment in life, you can write down what's in your mind. It could be thinking about past memories, finances, a hard problem at work, humming a song, or the strange person you saw on the train. It could also be some illegible gibberish, repeated many times. Who cares?

**The first step is:** You sit down and write. Voila. You're a writer. You are such a brave writer that you can write your innermost thoughts without fear. You even wrote the gibberish and laughed at it. 

**The second step is:** You decide which thoughts or ideas are worth publishing. You select them, edit them, and make them presentable to the readers. Ruthlessly, discard everything else.

Really, that’s all there is to writing. It’s no magic like everyone pretends.

When people talk about a writer's block, they are simply saying, **I am not able to write something worthy or aligned with my goal**. Of course, when you are writing a novel and your mind suddenly wanders to philosophy, you are not writing towards your goal. But you are still writing. Let the writing happen. What’s the worst that can happen?

My request for a beginner is to stay with the gibberish, as long as it takes. Keep writing your raw thoughts. If something good comes up and you feel safe enough, publish. If there is nothing good - all gore, bad, gross thoughts - trash it. Just write for yourself. 

I believe you would know yourself better after writing down your thoughts. Writer's block could be you simply resisting your own mind.
