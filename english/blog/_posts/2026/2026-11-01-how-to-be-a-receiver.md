title: How to Be a Receiver

_How to be a receiver?_ 

To realize that you’re a receiver and not interrupt the reception. 

_How do you realize it?_

Tune into your receiving conditions. It could be writing, drawing, composing music, playing with your kids, or sitting alone in the mountains. Figure out where and what makes you feel more creative. Be there and do that as frequently as you can. In most cases, when you allow your mind to wander freely, you’ll likely figure it out.

_If there is a receiver, there must be a sender, right? I can see and feel the receiver in flesh and blood. But I can’t even imagine a sender. How do I believe there is a sender?_

Should there be one? This question arises from the association of reception with religion or God.

There is a group of astronomers and scientists searching for aliens across the cosmos. This field is called SETI (Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence).

Just like us, they don’t know: 

1. If aliens exist
2. If they are sending information
3. If we send information, whether they would have the intelligence to receive and interpret it

What do these SETI scientists do? 

They point radio telescopes towards selected regions of the sky, collect electromagnetic signals, and analyze them for artificial signals. Until they have credible, independently verified evidence, they are not going to accept the existence of aliens. At the same time, they will not stop receiving and analyzing the electromagnetic signals.

This is very similar to our search for a sender. Isn’t it?

All I’m asking is to tune into your receiving conditions as frequently as you can. What you do with your transmission is up to you. Attribute it to God and become a believer, sure. Analyze it, question it, debate it, publish it, sure. Simply trash it, sure. Just explore what it feels like to receive, without bothering about the sender.

**Would you?**
