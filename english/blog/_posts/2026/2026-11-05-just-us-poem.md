title: Just Us
---

My heart yearns.  

Could I be yours,  
as you're mine,  
in here and now?  

Forgive this world  
that caged our love.  
Let's find another  
to forge our love.  

No gods.  
No commandments.  
Just us.  
Just for us.  

Let's eat all the apples  
and the snakes too.  

Let's do all the good.  
In all its glory, 
we love.  

Let's do all the bad.  
In all its shame, 
we love.  

There is no evil without love.  
There is no love without evil.  

The source of life is indifference.  

Let's live life.  
Let's be life.  

Come.  
I'm waiting!  

With love.
